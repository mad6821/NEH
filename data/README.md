## Data
1. **neh**: NEH grant data is exported from the publicly accessible database [data.gov](https://apps.neh.gov/open/data/). 
2. **tiger**: County shapefiles that contain geographic entity codes that can be linked to other demographic data from the [US Census Bureau](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/).
3. **arc**: County designations for the Appalachian region are from the [Appalachian Regional Commission (ARC)](https://data.arc.gov/data).
4. **bls**: Unemployment data is from the [Local Area Unemployment Statistics (LAUS)](https://www.bls.gov/lau/tables.htm) from the Bureau of Labor Statistics. 
5. **gaz**: The U.S. Gazetteer Files from the [US Census Bureau](https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/maps-data/data/gazetteer) provide a listing of all geographic areas for selected geographic area types. The files include geographic identifier codes, names, area measurements, and representative latitude and longitude coordinates.
6. **ipeds**: Carnegie classifications for higher education institutions are from the [Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System (IPEDS)](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/DataFiles.aspx?gotoReportId=7&fromIpeds=true&sid=aa7e6b22-7bde-4560-b773-08d66a1c3568&rtid=7).
7. **saipe**: Poverty data is from the [Small Area Income and Poverty Estimates (SAIPE)](https://www.census.gov/data-tools/demo/saipe/#/?s_state=13,01,21,24,28,37,39,45,42,47,51,54,36&s_county=&s_district=&s_geography=county&x_tableYears=2018,2019,2020,2021,2022) from the US Census Bureau. 
8. `analysis.csv`: is the cleaned and merged dataset used for the main analysis.
